## 🏛️ Exact Permit Drawing Specifications

- **Location**: Suite #9, 2905 Castleberry Rd, Cumming, GA 30040
- **Gross Area**: `1,056 SF` (`20'-11½"` Wide × `52'-1"` Long; `20'-4"` × `49'-10½"` Interior Clear)
- **Occupancy Classification**: Assembly (`38 Occupants Total` — 30 Dining Net) | IBC Type II-B
- **Coordinate System**: `1.0 Three.js Unit = 1.0 Foot` (Imperial Engineering Scale)

3. **`03 KITCHEN / KITCHENETTE` (`115 SF`)**: Accessed via double-acting traffic door `D`; finished in full-height white gloss FRP (`WT2`) and `8'-0"` washable ACT ceiling (`C4`) with **`K1` True Cooler**, **`K2` True Freezer**, **`K4` Hand Sink w/ Eyewash**, **`K5` Regency 3-Compartment Sink**, **`K7` SS Work Desk**, **`K8` Mop Sink**, and **`DS-1` Carrier 4-Way Ceiling Cassette**.

5. **`06 DRY STORAGE` (`115 SF`)**: Houses **`WH-1` A.O. Smith DRE-52 50-Gal 12.3 kW Electric Water Heater**, **`KE-47` Reverse Osmosis Filtration System**, **Existing 200A Panelboard `A`**, **New 60A Panelboard `B`**, and wire shelving (`K3`/`K6`).

## 🔧 Interactive Engineering Discipline Modes

Use the top-center **Engineering Discipline Selector** to switch between:
- **`Composite BIM`**: Full architectural, interior, and MEP view.
- **`Interior & Finishes`**: Highlights custom millwork, walnut slat wall (`WC2`), oyster & green subway tiles (`WT1`/`WC1`), coffee-bean ceiling clouds (`CC`), cove lighting (`LT8`), and equipment (`B1–B18`, `K1–K8`).
- **`Mechanical (HVAC)`**: X-ray view of **`(E) RTU-9`** (Carrier 4.0-Ton Rooftop Unit), `16"Ø`/`12"Ø`/`10"Ø` exposed galvanized spiral supply ducts, `S-1` 3-cone diffusers, `S-2` spiral double-deflection grilles, `14"×22"` return air duct, **`DS-1`/`HP-1`** (Carrier 1.0-Ton 4-Way Split System), **`EF-1`** (Greenheck 140 CFM Restroom Exhaust Fan), and **`AC-1`** (Powered Aire Drive-Thru Air Curtain).
- **`Electrical & Lighting`**: X-ray view of **200A Panelboard `A`** & **60A Panelboard `B`**, `208V`/`120V` EMT conduit runs, dedicated equipment drops, and scheduled luminaires (`LT1–LT8`, `EXL1–EXL3`).
- **`Plumbing & Gas`**: X-ray view of under-slab `4" SS` sanitary sewer, `3" GW` grease waste to the 1,500-gal grease interceptor, `5x FS-1` stainless floor sinks, `1-1/4" CW`/`HW` mains from `WH-1`, `1/2" RO` filtered water loop from `KE-47`, and `3/4" G` rooftop natural gas line.
